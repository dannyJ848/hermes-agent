# Epistemic Trust in AI: 3-Level Justified Trust Model (Kreze, 2026)

**Source:** Philosophy and Governance, No. 1(17), 2026. DOI: 10.70651/3041-248X/2026.1.19
**Author:** Oleksii Kreze, State University of Trade and Economics

## Core Thesis
Epistemic trust in AI requires a transition from absolute trust to critical scientific verification using a 3-level logical model producing "justified trust."

## The 3-Level Justified Trust Model

### Level 1: Reproducibility Check
- Verify AI outputs can be reproduced independently
- Check against known ground truth

### Level 2: Comparative Analysis
- Cross-reference AI results with alternative sources
- Interpret differences and identify conflicts

### Level 3: Integration Gate
- Only after Levels 1+2 pass with no issues, integrate into trusted knowledge
- Human remains the "active arbiter of truth"

## Key Limits of Epistemic Trust in AI
1. **Opacity**: "Black box" problem, algorithmic hallucination, illusion of explanations
2. **Training data dependency**: AI outputs limited by training corpus
3. **Domain boundedness**: AI only effective within its design scope

## Risks Identified
- Impossibility of full process understanding
- Limited verification of results
- Dependence on data quality
- Reduction of critical thinking
- Formation of "technological authority"
- Cognitive deskilling
- Epistemic isolation
- Reduced discovery

## Relevance to Cerebrum/Evey
- Our F-G-R Trust Tuple partially implements Level 1 (grounding = reproducibility)
- We lack Level 2 (comparative analysis between competing memories)
- Level 3 (integration gate) maps to our semantic consolidation — but we should add an explicit conflict resolution step
- The "human as arbiter" principle supports our design: Danny controls memory, Evey proposes

## Actionable Improvement
Add **conflict resolution** to Cerebrum: when storing a new memory that semantically contradicts an existing one, run a comparative analysis (Level 2) before integration. Flag conflicts for human review rather than silently adding competing facts.

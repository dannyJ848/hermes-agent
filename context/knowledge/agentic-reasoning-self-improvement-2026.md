# Agentic Reasoning & Self-Improving Agents (2026 Survey)

## Key Paper: Agentic Reasoning for Large Language Models (arXiv 2601.12538, Jan 2026)
Three-layer framework:
1. **Foundational Reasoning** — planning, tool use, search in stable environments
2. **Self-Evolving Reasoning** — agents refine capabilities through feedback, memory, and self-modification
3. **Multi-Agent Reasoning** — collaborative and competitive agent systems

Awesome-Agentic-Reasoning repo (1.2k stars): curated papers covering planning, tool use, search, self-evolution, memory, multi-agent systems.

## HyperAgents (Meta/UBC/Oxford/NYU, Mar 2026)
- Metacognitive self-improvement: agents modify their own modification process
- Transferred strategies from robotics/paper-review to Olympiad math grading
- imp@50 = 0.630 vs human-engineered systems scoring 0.0
- Cross-domain transfer of self-improvement strategies

## Self-Improvement Landscape (2026)
- **Evolutionary**: AlphaEvolve, ShinkaEvolve
- **RL methods**: SWE-RL, SAGE
- **Memory systems**: Mem0, MemOS, SimpleMem
- **Production**: Meta REA, Cognition/Devin, Karpathy's autoresearch loop
- METR benchmarks: autonomous task length doubling every 4 months (accelerated from 7 months)
- 50% reliability time horizon: ~50 minutes (up from 15 min a year ago)

## Key Insight for SOMA/Hermes
The self-evolving layer maps directly to our cerebrum/tip distillation pipeline. Cross-domain strategy transfer (HyperAgents) suggests our distilled tips should generalize beyond their extraction domain — test this hypothesis.

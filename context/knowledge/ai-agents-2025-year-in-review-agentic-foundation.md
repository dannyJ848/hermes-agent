# AI Agents: 2025 Year in Review

## Key Milestones (from The Conversation / Carnegie Mellon):
- 2025 was the decisive shift year — AI agents moved from theory to infrastructure
- Definition shifted from academic "perceive, reason, act" to Anthropic's framing: "LLMs capable of using software tools and taking autonomous action"
- Key inflection: Anthropic released MCP (Model Context Protocol) in late 2024
- January 2025: DeepSeek-R1 open-weight release disrupted markets, intensified global competition
- Late 2025: Linux Foundation created the **Agentic AI Foundation** — signaling effort to establish shared standards
- Major labs (OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, xAI) released larger high-performance models throughout 2025
- Chinese tech companies also competing heavily

## Implications:
- Agentic AI Foundation under Linux Foundation = significant for standardization
- MCP becoming the de facto standard for tool connectivity
- Agent space moving from "can we build agents?" to "how do we standardize and govern them?"

# AI Reasoning Advances 2026: Chain-of-Thought, Theory of Mind, and Dual-Process Models

## Key Findings

### Chain-of-Thought (CoT) Breakthrough
- Zero-shot CoT boosting text-davinci-002 from 17.7% → 78.7% on MultiArith (+61pp)
- GSM8K improvement: 10.4% → 40.7% (+30.3pp)
- Three mechanisms explain gains: decomposition, self-verification, attention redistribution

### New Prompting Techniques (2026)
- **DR-CoT (Dynamic Recursive Chain of Thought)** — recursive self-refinement of reasoning chains
- "Think step by step" is now considered obsolete for dedicated reasoning models
- Reasoning models struggle to control their CoT for monitorability (OpenAI research)

### Theory of Mind in LLMs
- GPT-4 passes 49.7% of social reasoning games (humans: 66%)
- False-belief tasks (Sally-Anne test) — models show emerging ToM capabilities
- Still below human performance on social reasoning

### System 1 vs System 2 Parallel
- Standard LLM inference ≈ System 1 (fast pattern matching)
- CoT + self-consistency + verification ≈ System 2 (deliberate reasoning)
- Techniques: CoT prompting, self-consistency (multiple reasoning paths), tree-of-thought, ReAct

### 2026 Model Landscape
- DeepSeek V4: 1 trillion parameters, native multimodality
- Frontier models: native multimodality within single foundation model is the new standard

## Implications for Agent Systems
1. **Multi-path reasoning** (self-consistency) should be used for high-stakes decisions
2. **DR-CoT** could improve tool-calling accuracy by recursively refining plans
3. **ToM capabilities** enable better multi-agent coordination
4. **Controllability concern** — reasoning models may develop hidden reasoning patterns

# BioMCP Literature Scan: 3D Anatomy Education Apps (Feb 2026)

*Scanned: 2026-04-02 22:20 CDT via BioMCP*

## Key Papers

### 1. Smartphone Photogrammetry for 3D Anatomy (PMID 41736195)
- **Citation:** Phukwantong et al., *Clin Anat*, 2026-02-24, DOI: 10.1002/ca.70100
- **Finding:** Compared Qlone vs Polycam for creating 3D/AR anatomy models from cadaveric specimens using iPhone 12. Polycam significantly superior in realism, resolution, shape fidelity, and educational value. Models rated "good" to "excellent" across realism, clarity, completeness, and educational value.
- **SOMA Insight:** Photogrammetry from cadaveric specimens is a viable pipeline for creating 3D anatomy content. Polycam (iOS) is the best tool. Could complement Z-Anatomy Blender models with photorealistic textures captured from real specimens.

### 2. 3D Reproductive Anatomy Mobile App Evaluation (PMID 41793998)
- **Citation:** Smyth et al., *Midwifery*, 2026-02-21, DOI: 10.1016/j.midw.2026.104749
- **Finding:** "Road to Birth" 3D multimodal interactive app evaluated with 178 midwifery + medical students across AU and UK. System Usability Scale score: 80/100 (good). 5-point Likert acceptability: 4.58. Students agreed app improved reproductive anatomy understanding. 12-week self-study period.
- **SOMA Insight:** Validates market demand for mobile 3D anatomy education. SUS score of 80 is the target for SOMA's UX. The self-study model (12 weeks, no instructor) matches SOMA's use case for community health education. Bilingual aspect would be a differentiator.

### 3. Cadaveric Brain Dissection Preservation via App (PMID 41636833)
- **Citation:** Europe PMC, 2026-02-04
- **SOMA Insight:** AR/3D can preserve dissection learning experiences digitally — relevant for SOMA's education branch.

## Market Validation

- Self-study model validated as effective for medical education

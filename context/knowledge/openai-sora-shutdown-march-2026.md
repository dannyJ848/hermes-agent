# OpenAI Shuts Down Sora — March 24, 2026

## Details
- OpenAI discontinued Sora (AI video generation) just 6 months after public launch
- 1M+ downloads in first week, but active users collapsed to <500K
- Burn rate: $15M/day in compute costs
- Lifetime revenue: only $2.1M total
- Planned $1B Disney partnership unraveled
- Compute being redirected to next-gen "Spud" language model and enterprise tools ahead of anticipated IPO

## Signal
- Shows economics of consumer AI video generation are still challenging
- Efficiency-first approach (TurboQuant) matters more than raw capability
- Compute allocation shifting toward enterprise productivity and next-gen language models

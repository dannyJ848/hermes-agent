# Self-Improving AI Agents: 2026 Landscape

## Key Finding: HyperAgents (Meta/UBC/Oxford/NYU, March 2026)
- Transferred self-improvement strategies from one domain (robotics, paper review) to a novel domain (Olympiad math grading)
- Scored imp@50 = 0.630 vs human-engineered systems scoring 0.0
- Metacognitive self-improvement: agents modify their own modification process

## METR Benchmark Data
- AI agent task completion horizon doubling every 7 months (R²=0.98, 6-year trend)
- 2024-2025: accelerated to doubling every 4 months
- Current 50% reliability time horizon: ~50 minutes (was <15 a year ago)

## Major Paradigms (2026)
1. **Evolutionary:** AlphaEvolve (Google DeepMind), ShinkaEvolve
2. **RL-based:** SWE-RL, SAGE
3. **Memory systems:** Mem0, MemOS, SimpleMem
4. **Production:** Meta REA, Cognition/Devin, Karpathy's autoresearch loop

## Lineage: ADAS → DGM → HyperAgents
- ADAS: Automated Design of Agentic Systems
- DGM: Deep Generative Models for agent improvement
- HyperAgents: Meta-learning across domains

## Relevance to Hermes/Evey
- Evey's distillation pipeline (tips → distilled_tips) is a simpler version of metacognitive self-improvement
- The cerebrum knowledge graph (213 nodes, 539 edges) serves as a primitive memory system
- Potential upgrade: implement cross-domain transfer of improvement strategies (like HyperAgents)

# AI Tools Landscape: Gemma 4, JetBrains Survey, NVIDIA Vera Rubin (April 2026)

## Google Gemma 4 (Launched April 2)
- **Apache 2.0 license**, built from same research as Gemini 3
- 31B Dense variant ranks **3rd on Arena AI's open model leaderboard**
- AMD day-zero support across Instinct GPUs, Radeon GPUs, Ryzen AI
- Supported via: vLLM, SGLang, llama.cpp, Ollama, LM Studio

## JetBrains AI Pulse Survey (Jan 2026, 10K+ developers)
- **90% of professional developers** now use at least one AI tool regularly
- **GitHub Copilot** leads work adoption
- **Claude Code** has risen to **share second place** alongside Copilot — each used by **18%** of developers
- Claude Code scored **80.8% on SWE-bench Verified** — highest published score for complex debugging

## NVIDIA Vera Rubin Platform
- In full production; cloud providers in H2 2026

- Targets: **10x reduction in inference token cost**, **4x reduction in GPUs for MoE training**
- Microsoft's **Fairwater AI superfactories** — hundreds of thousands of Vera Rubin Superchips

# Meta-Reasoning in Autonomous Agents: Performance Gains Across Benchmarks and Models

**Citation:** Talukdar, W., Biswas, A., Shankar, G., Shinde, V., & Parekh, G. (2026). "Meta-reasoning in autonomous agents: performance gains across benchmarks and models." *Academia AI and Applications*, 2(1). DOI: 10.20935/AcadAI8229

**Published:** March 31, 2026

## Key Findings
- Agents equipped with meta-reasoning frameworks show **31.2% statistically significant performance improvement** across benchmarks
- Meta-reasoning enables agents to self-assess, plan reasoning strategies, and adaptively select approaches per task
- Tested across multiple model families and benchmark suites

# EvoMap vs Hermes Agent License Dispute (April 2026)

**Date:** April 17, 2026
**Priority:** HIGH — directly involves Nous Research / Hermes Agent

## Summary
Chinese AI team EvoMap changed its Evolver engine license from MIT to GPLv3 after accusing Nous Research of copying its self-evolving architecture for Hermes Agent.

## Key Facts
- EvoMap claims "high-level isomorphism" between Hermes Agent's self-evolving function and their Evolver engine
- Alleged "one-to-one correspondence" in the 10-step main loop
- Claims "systematic replacement of 12 groups of terms" with zero attribution
- EvoMap's Evolver: ~2,325 GitHub stars (launched Feb 1, 2026)
- Hermes Agent: 85,000+ stars in under 2 months; deployed by Tencent Cloud & Alibaba Cloud

## Teknium's Response
Co-founder Teknium responded: "I have literally never heard of this person, their project, or anything they are doing in my life"

## Root Cause Theory
EvoMap suggested AI-assisted coding tools may have unknowingly replicated their architecture — "code washing" cost near zero.

## Industry Implications
- Raises questions about AI-generated code and open-source attribution
- EvoMap calls for new open-source protocols tailored to AI-assisted development
- Governance frameworks needed to protect not just code but ideas and creative thinking

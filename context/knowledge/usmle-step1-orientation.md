# USMLE Step 1 — Orientation Brief (2026)

## Exam Format
- **Type:** Computer-based, 280 MCQs, 7 blocks x 60 min, single 8-hour day
- **Location:** Prometric test centers
- **Scoring:** Pass/Fail since Jan 2022. Internal passing threshold: 196/300
- **Failure consequences:** Permanently recorded on transcript. Residency programs see it. MUST pass first attempt.

## Pass Rates (2024, post-P/F era)
- US MD: 89% (was 95% pre-P/F)
- US DO: 86%
- IMG: 70%
- First-time takers overall: 82%
- ~1 in 9 US MD students now fail — the "Pass/Fail effect"

## Core Resources (Gold Standard)
1. **UWorld Qbank** — primary question bank, ~80-120 Q/day during dedicated
2. **First Aid for the USMLE Step 1** — the bible, annotate everything into it
3. **Pathoma** — pathology videos + book, especially Chapters 1-3 (highest yield)
4. **Sketchy Medical** — microbiology & pharmacology via visual mnemonics
5. **Anki (AnKing deck)** — spaced repetition, 100-200 cards/day minimum
6. **Boards & Beyond (B&B)** — comprehensive video lectures for weak areas
7. **NBME Practice Exams** — take every 2 weeks, score tracking
8. **UWorld Self-Assessments (UWSA)** — predictive of real performance
9. **Free 120** — official USMLE practice, use in final days for interface familiarity
10. **AMBOSS** — supplementary Qbank, good for ethics/biostats

## 3-Week Intensive Strategy (Danny's Timeline)
Given ~23 days, this is a compressed "4-week" plan (borderline aggressive):
- **Requires:** significant pre-dedicated prep already done (UWorld first pass, First Aid review)
- **Day structure:** 10-12 hrs/day, 1 rest day/week
- **Week 1:** Foundation reinforcement — 2 UWorld blocks/day timed random, Pathoma Ch 1-3, First Aid rapid review of weak systems, Anki 200/day
- **Week 2:** Ramp — 3 UWorld blocks/day, NBME practice exam, target weak systems identified by UWorld analytics, Sketchy for micro/pharm gaps
- **Week 3:** Peak & taper — Full-length practice exams, UWorld incorrect/marked review, Free 120, First Aid Rapid Review section, rest day before exam
- **Critical:** NBME baseline ASAP to identify where the gaps are

## Key Study Principles
1. **Active > passive:** Questions > re-reading. UWorld is king.
2. **Pathology dominates:** Nearly half the exam. Pathoma Ch 1-3 are non-negotiable.
3. **Spaced repetition:** Anki every single day, no exceptions.
4. **Simulate exam conditions:** Full-length practice tests under real timing.
5. **Error classification:** Wrong answers → knowledge gap vs misread vs reasoning failure.
6. **Sleep is non-negotiable:** Consolidation happens during sleep.

# Manus AI Agent — Autonomous Multi-Agent Breakthrough (March-April 2025)

**Date:** March-April 2025

## Summary
Manus, created by a Chinese startup, is a comprehensive multi-agent AI system positioned as moving beyond chatbots to full autonomous task execution. Invitation-only access.

## Architecture
- **Multi-agent system**: Functions like a CEO overseeing specialized sub-agents
- **Planner agent** → breaks tasks into subtasks
- **Sub-agents** → select from 29 integrated tools
- **Synthesized output** → combined results

## Core Innovations
1. **Dynamic Task Decomposition Algorithm** — autonomously decomposes complex instructions into execution paths
2. **Chain of Thought Injection** — agents reflect and update plans through reasoning rounds

## Benchmark: GAIA
- Manus scored 86.5% (humans typically score ~92%)

## Capabilities
- Travel itineraries, financial analysis, education content, database compilation, insurance comparisons, supplier sourcing, presentations

## The "Wrapper" Debate
- Critics dismiss as a wrapper; defenders argue execution quality (UI, evals, fine-tuning, multi-agent design) is the differentiator
- Users can inspect, customize, or replace individual sub-agents

## Limitations
- Coordination difficulty at scale
- Vulnerable to replication by competitors

**Priority:** MEDIUM — significant agent framework development
